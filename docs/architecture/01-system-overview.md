# Operix - System Overview

## Overview

Operix is an **Enterprise Asset & Operations Management Platform (EAOMP)** designed to help organizations manage assets, maintenance, inventory, and operational processes from a single platform.

Version 1 focuses on building a modern **Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS)**, with future expansion into broader enterprise operations.

---

## Purpose

Operix aims to replace manual and disconnected operational processes with a centralized, scalable, and maintainable platform that improves efficiency, visibility, and decision-making.

---

## Vision

Build a modular enterprise platform that evolves from a CMMS into a complete Enterprise Asset & Operations Management Platform.

---

## Objectives

- Centralize operational data
- Improve asset lifecycle management
- Reduce equipment downtime
- Streamline maintenance workflows
- Improve reporting and analytics
- Provide a scalable foundation for future modules

---

## Target Users
